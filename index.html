How we work

From day one
to tenfold

The same playbook behind every Tenfold game, adapted to each project's genre, audience and goals.

01
Step 01

Concept

We start with what players are searching for right now and find the angle that makes an idea instantly clickable.

  • Trend researchWhat's rising on the platform and why it works
  • Core loopThe 30-second action players will repeat for hours
  • Hook & titleA name and promise that read in one glance
  • ScopeA realistic plan for what ships first
Step 02

Prototype

A playable version fast. If the core loop isn't fun with grey boxes, no amount of polish will save it.

  • Playable in daysCore mechanic first, everything else later
  • PlaytestsReal players, real reactions
  • Kill or keepWe drop ideas that don't feel great
  • Feel passCamera, sounds, hit feedback
Step 03

Build

Full production: systems, content, UI and economy, built on clean code that's easy to update after launch.

  • Server-first systemsSecure, exploit-resistant game logic
  • Data & savesReliable player data that never gets lost
  • ProgressionRewards paced for day 1, 7 and 30
  • Polished UIClear, bright, made for mobile first
Step 04

Launch

Thumbnails, icons, store page and short-form content come together to give the game the strongest possible first week.

  • Thumbnails & iconTested for click-through
  • Store pageDescription, tags and age rating done right
  • Shorts & TikToksGameplay clips with real hooks
  • Day-one monitoringBugs fixed within hours, not days
Step 05

Live ops

Launch is the start. We read the numbers every week and ship updates, events and content to keep players coming back.

  • Weekly analyticsRetention, session length, conversion
  • Update calendarFresh content on a steady rhythm
  • Events & codesReasons to come back today
  • RebalancingEconomy tuned from real player data
